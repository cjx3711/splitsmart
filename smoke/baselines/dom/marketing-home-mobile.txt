  - region "What's in it":
    - heading "What's in it" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The same jobs Splitwise does, minus the upsell that shows up while you're adding a bill. Plus a whole bunch of small fixes to things that have irritated me for years.
    - heading "Same as Splitwise" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: Groups, friends, and one-off expenses
      - listitem: "Six split types: equal, exact, percent, shares, adjustment, itemised"
      - listitem: Settle up, with suggested transfers if the group wants to simplify
      - listitem: An activity feed for the groups and expenses you're on
      - listitem: Offline support
      - listitem: 100+ currencies
    - heading "Improvements" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: As many expenses a day as you want
      - listitem: Clicking just outside the add-expense box doesn't dump the draft
      - listitem: Currencies with no decimals don't show any, so 3000 JPY is 3000 JPY, not 3000.00
      - listitem: Mixed balances show a live ≈ estimate, and you can convert them into one currency at <RELDATE>'s rate
      - listitem: A currency picker that puts the ones you frequently use at the top
      - listitem: Itemised bills, where each line has its own sharers and tax and tip get spread proportionally
      - listitem: Splitwise import, and your API key is used for the request and then dropped
      - listitem: Installable as a PWA, and the ledger stays on the device
      - listitem: A JSON API at /api/v1, with a bearer token from Settings
